FAQ

Questions

Is it a website or an app?

A Mac app. Your leads, mailboxes and API keys live on your computer, not on our servers.

Which Macs?

Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) today. Intel and Windows are planned.

Will my emails land in spam?

No tool can promise that. Lcino ramps volume slowly, enforces gaps, verifies addresses and pauses on bounces. Use a separate sending domain, never your main one.

Do I need my own email accounts?

Yes. Connect your Google Workspace, Outlook or SMTP mailboxes.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. No card and no time limit; it is capped on mailboxes, emails and audits.

Who owns my data?

You do. It stays on your Mac, secrets are encrypted on disk, and daily backups are kept locally.
