Acceptable Use & Anti-spam Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Lcino Outreach, a product of Lcino

Cold email is legal and useful when it is relevant, honest and easy to stop. This policy keeps Lcino Outreach for that kind of outreach.

1. You must

  • Contact businesses and professionals about something relevant to their work (B2B), not consumers at random.
  • Identify yourself truthfully: real sender name, real reply address, accurate subject lines.
  • Include your valid postal address or the details required by the laws that apply to you.
  • Offer a working way to opt out and honour every opt-out immediately, and no later than 10 days.
  • Follow the law where you and your recipients are, including CAN-SPAM, GDPR/PECR, CASL and similar rules. Some countries require prior consent for cold email; it is your job to know which.
  • Send from domains and mailboxes you own, preferably a separate sending domain, and warm up gradually.
  • Verify addresses and stop on high bounce or complaint rates.

2. You must not

  • Send spam, bulk unsolicited consumer marketing, or messages with misleading headers, sender identity or subjects.
  • Use harvested, rented or purchased lists of people who have not been given a lawful basis to be contacted, or scrape data you are not allowed to scrape.
  • Send phishing, malware, scams, fake audits, deceptive claims or illegal, hateful, adult or harassing content.
  • Impersonate another person or company, or misuse a brand you do not represent.
  • Bypass the App’s safety limits (daily ramp, gaps, bounce brakes) or the opt-out handling.
  • Resell or redistribute the contact data supplied with the App.
  • Use the App to harm, stalk or target private individuals.

3. Our safety features

The App ramps volume gradually, spaces emails per mailbox, pauses campaigns when bounces rise, and records opt-outs. They help you stay within good practice. They do not replace your own responsibility.

4. Enforcement

If we receive credible complaints or find a breach, we may warn you, suspend or end your licence without refund, and cooperate with authorities and providers. Report abuse through lcino.com.
