Refund Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · LcinoOutreach, a product of Lcino

14-day money-back guarantee. If you buy a paid plan for the first time and it is not for you, ask for a refund within 14 days and you will get your money back.

1. Free plan

The Free plan costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund. Please try it before you buy.

2. First purchase: 14 days

Your first payment for a paid plan (monthly or yearly) is refundable in full if you request it within 14 days of the payment date. We do not ask you to justify the request.

3. Renewals

You can cancel at any time and will not be charged again. Renewal payments are generally non-refundable, because you can cancel before the renewal date. If you were charged for a renewal and forgot to cancel, contact us within 3 days and we will review it kindly.

4. Upgrades and downgrades

If you upgrade mid-term, the unused part of the previous plan is credited toward the new one. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal; we do not refund the difference.

5. When we may refuse a refund

  • The account was used to break our Acceptable Use policy (for example spam or abuse).
  • Repeated refund requests, or a request after the 14-day window for a first purchase, unless the law says otherwise.
  • Costs outside our control, such as third-party API, AI, verification, domain or mailbox fees you paid to other providers.
  • Deliverability or reply results. Sending outcomes depend on your domains, mailboxes, content and recipients, and are not guaranteed.

6. Technical problems

If the App does not work as described on your Mac, tell us first. We will try to fix it, and if we cannot, we will refund you even outside the 14 days.

7. How to ask

Contact us using the support details in the footer or via lcino.com, with the email used for the purchase. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. Please contact us before opening a chargeback so we can solve it faster.

8. Your legal rights

This policy does not affect any statutory rights you have as a consumer where you live.
