Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Lcino Outreach, a product of Lcino

The short version. Lcino Outreach runs on your own Mac. Your leads, mailboxes, API keys and campaigns stay on your computer. We do not read them, sell them or train models on them.

1. Who we are

“Lcino Outreach” is a software product developed and published by Lcino (“Lcino”, “we”, “us”). This policy covers this website and the Lcino Outreach desktop application (the “App”).

2. What stays on your computer

  • Leads, lists, audits, campaigns, sequences, replies, notes and pipeline data.
  • Mailbox credentials and the API keys you add (stored encrypted on your disk).
  • Local backups of your workspace.

We have no access to this data. When you connect a mailbox, an AI provider, YouTube/Google Maps data sources or an email-verification service, the App talks to that provider directly from your Mac, under that provider’s own terms and privacy policy.

4. Contact data inside the App

The App may include a database of business contacts (such as business names, work emails and company details) compiled from publicly available business sources. It is provided to help you find relevant businesses. You decide who to contact and you are responsible for doing so lawfully (see our Acceptable Use & Anti-spam policy). If you appear in that data and want to be removed, contact us and we will remove you promptly.

5. How we use data

  • To provide, secure and improve the App and website.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To answer your requests and send essential service messages.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your workspace content for advertising or to train AI models.

6. Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run the business (payment processing, hosting, email delivery for our own messages), under agreements that limit their use of it; when required by law; or in a business transfer, with notice to you.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data in other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

8. Retention

Account and billing records are kept while your account is active and as long as tax or accounting law requires. Support messages are kept for up to 24 months. Everything inside the App stays on your computer until you delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR or similar laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights; we respond within 30 days.

10. Security

Secrets in the App are encrypted at rest, and the App limits repeated login attempts. No system is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for protecting your own computer, backups and mailbox passwords.

11. Children

Lcino Outreach is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
