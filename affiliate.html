Affiliate Program

Effective 3 November 2026 · Lcino Outreach, a product of Lcino

Earn 20% commission on every payment from customers you refer, for the first 12 months of their subscription.

How it works

  • Apply and receive your personal referral link.
  • Share it with freelancers, marketers and agencies who would benefit from audit-first outreach.
  • When someone clicks your link and buys a paid plan within 30 days, you earn 20% of each payment they make for their first 12 months.
  • Commissions become payable 30 days after the customer’s payment (after the refund window) once your balance reaches USD 50.

Program rules

  • Be honest. No fake reviews, false income claims or misleading comparisons.
  • No spam, no cold-emailing people you have no right to contact, and no bidding on the “Lcino” or “Lcino Outreach” brand names in paid search.
  • No self-referrals or referrals through coupon sites without approval.
  • Refunded or charged-back payments do not earn commission, and already-paid commission on them can be deducted.
  • Disclose your affiliate relationship clearly where the law requires, for example “I earn a commission if you buy through my link”.
  • We may change or end the program, or remove an affiliate who breaks these rules, with notice. Earned and payable commission will still be paid.
  • You are responsible for your own taxes.

Apply

Applications open with the first paid release. Use the contact details in the footer or lcino.com and write “Affiliate” in your message.
